pip open your desk

What it stores

Without an account

Everything stays in your browser. Your tasks, your days, your settings and your session history are kept in that browser's local storage and go nowhere else. Clearing site data removes them, so export a copy if they matter.

With an account

Your email address, and the same data as above: lists, tags, tasks, day blocks and finished sessions. That is the whole of it. There is no tracking, no analytics and no advertising, and nothing is shared with anyone except as described under “The AI” below, if you use it.

It is stored in a Supabase project, and row-level security means each account can only ever read and write its own rows.

The AI

Only if you connect it (settings → AI). To plan your evening and your week, or to read a note into tasks, pip sends the names, deadlines and sizes of your open tasks, this week's calendar events, your bedtime, and whatever you wrote under “about you, right now” or told it for today or this week — never your email or password.

By default that goes to Puter, through your own free Puter account, which passes it to the AI models (Google's Gemini and OpenAI's GPT). Puter's script is only loaded once you reach for the AI. If this copy of pip runs its own AI server instead, it goes there and on to Groq and Google's Gemini. Google may use what is sent to its free tier to improve its products, so leave out anything you would not want read. Switch the AI off and nothing is sent at all; pip plans with its own rules instead.

Your calendar

If you connect one, its private link is kept in your account and only ever used to read it. Its events are shown to you and planned around; they are not copied into your data.

Taking it with you

Export in settings writes the lot to a JSON file you keep. It works signed in or out and depends on nothing but your browser.

Deleting it

Delete account on your account page (the person button, top right of the desk) removes your account and everything attached to it. It cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy. Without an account, clearing your browser's site data is the same thing.

Email

Your address is used to sign you in and to send a reset link if you ask for one. Nothing else is ever sent to it.

pip is free, has no paid tier, and is not planning one. The source is on GitHub.
