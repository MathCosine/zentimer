pip open your desk

What it stores

Without an account

Everything stays in your browser. Your tasks, your days, your settings and your session history are kept in that browser's local storage and go nowhere else. Clearing site data removes them, so export a copy if they matter.

With an account

Your email address, and the same data as above: lists, tags, tasks, day blocks and finished sessions. That is the whole of it. There is no tracking, no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is shared with anyone.

It is stored in a Supabase project, and row-level security means each account can only ever read and write its own rows.

Taking it with you

Export in settings writes the lot to a JSON file you keep. It works signed in or out and depends on nothing but your browser.

Deleting it

Delete account in the account panel removes your account and everything attached to it. It cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy. Without an account, clearing your browser's site data is the same thing.

Email

Your address is used to sign you in and to send a reset link if you ask for one. Nothing else is ever sent to it.

pip is free, has no paid tier, and is not planning one. The source is on GitHub.
